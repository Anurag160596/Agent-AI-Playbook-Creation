CARD SERVICES — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document: SOP-CS-014  |  Owner: Fraud & Disputes  |  Last reviewed: 2026-01-30

Purpose
This procedure covers how a contact centre agent handles a customer request to
block a debit or credit card that has been lost, stolen, or compromised.

Procedure
1. Before taking any action on the account, the agent must verify the identity of
   the caller. Verification requires the customer to correctly provide at least
   two of: full name, date of birth, and the last four digits of the card. If the
   customer cannot be verified, the agent must NOT proceed and should follow the
   failed-verification escalation process.

2. Once the customer is verified, ask the customer for the reason they want the
   card blocked (for example: lost, stolen, or suspected fraud). Record the reason
   in the case notes.

3. A one-time passcode (OTP) is sent to the mobile number on file. The agent must
   ask the customer to read back the OTP and must confirm it matches before any
   block is placed. This step is mandatory and cannot be skipped for any reason.

4. After the OTP is confirmed, the agent places the block on the card in the card
   management system. The block is immediate and irreversible.

5. Ask the customer whether they would like a replacement card issued to the
   address on file. If yes, raise a replacement-card request and inform them that
   the new card arrives in 5–7 business days. If no, no further action is needed.

6. Finally, inform the customer that the block is now active and read the standard
   fraud-liability disclosure to them before ending the call.

Notes
- Identity verification (step 1) and OTP confirmation (step 3) are regulatory
  requirements and are audited monthly. A block placed without a confirmed OTP is
  a reportable compliance breach.
